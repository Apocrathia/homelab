# Design

Open-source creative design and CAD tools.

> **Navigation**: [← Back to Apps README](../README.md)

## Applications

### [BenchUp3D](./benchup/README.md)

SketchUp-style 3D modeler for woodworking in the browser.

### [CADCraft](./cadcraft/README.md)

Open-source CAD, parametric solid modeling in the browser.

### [Craft Anything](./craft-anything/README.md)

Understand how any software works, then craft your own.

### [DesignCraft](./designcraft/README.md)

Page and layout design editor in the browser.

### [EffectCraft](./effectcraft/README.md)

Motion graphics and compositing in the browser.

### [FigCraft](./figcraft/README.md)

Interface design and prototyping in the browser.

### [FilmCraft](./filmcraft/README.md)

Nonlinear video editor in the browser.

### [Klecks](./klecks/README.md)

Web-based painting tool powering Kleki.com.

### [LightCraft](./lightcraft/README.md)

Non-destructive photo editor in the browser.

### [MaterialCraft](./materialcraft/README.md)

Node-based material designer in the browser.

### [PdfCraft](./pdfcraft/README.md)

PDF reader and editor in the browser.

### [PhotoCraft](./photocraft/README.md)

Photo editor in the browser.

### [SolidCraft](./solidcraft/README.md)

Parametric 3D CAD in the browser, an independent project from Tangency Labs.

### [SoundCraft](./soundcraft/README.md)

Digital audio workstation (DAW) in the browser.

### [tldraw](./tldraw/README.md)

Multiplayer whiteboard — realtime collaboration, room-based scenes.

### [TextureCraft](./texturecraft/README.md)

3D texture painting workflow in the browser.

### [VectorCraft](./vectorcraft/README.md)

Vector art tool in the browser.
